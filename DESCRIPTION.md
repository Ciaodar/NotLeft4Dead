[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
[b]Not Left 4 Dead (NL4D)[/b] is a [b]100% Zero-Harmony-Patch[/b] mod for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord ([b]v1.5.7+[/b]) that expands the new [b]Battle Wreckage[/b] mechanic with dynamic, lore-rich companion rescues.

When you finish investigating a land or naval battle wreckage, a wounded survivor clinging to life amidst the carnage may crawl out of the debris. Question them about the battle, learn which lord abandoned them, check their story in the Encyclopedia via clickable links, and decide whether to recruit them into your company on the spot or send them to recover at the nearest tavern - where they will remember your mercy and join you for [b]free[/b] whenever you meet again.
[/color][/size]

[size=4][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
[list=1]
[*]Download [b]NL4D-Latest.zip[/b] (or install via Vortex / your mod manager).
[*]Extract the [b]NL4D[/b] folder into your Bannerlord [b]Modules[/b] directory: [i]Mount & Blade II Bannerlord\Modules\NL4D\[/i]
[*]In the Bannerlord Launcher, enable [b]Not Left 4 Dead[/b] (place below [b]Mod Configuration Menu v5[/b] if you use MCM).
[*]Safe to install mid-campaign and works without Harmony or MCM.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]
[list]
[*][b]Zero-Harmony-Patch Integration (v1.5.7 Battle Wreckage):[/b] Hooks cleanly into Bannerlord v1.5.7's native BattleWreckage system without a single Harmony patch. Triggers immediately when the 9-hour wreckage investigation bar finishes.
[*][b]Real Battle Context & Clickable Encyclopedia Links:[/b] Pulls the actual commanding lords, opposing commanders, kingdoms/clans, total casualties, and elapsed hours from the wreckage. Every lord, faction, and settlement mentioned in dialogue is a clickable Encyclopedia link.
[*][b]Interactive Lore Dialogue Hub:[/b] Before deciding their fate, ask the survivor in any order:
  - [i]"What actually happened here? Tell me of the battle."[/i]
  - [i]"Who are you really? What is your story?"[/i]
  - [i]"What happened to you? How bad are your wounds?"[/i]
[*][b]Custom Encyclopedia Biography & Met Status:[/b] Survivors are automatically marked as met ([i]HasMet[/i]) and receive a custom Encyclopedia biography recording who abandoned them, who they fought, and that you rescued them.
[*][b]Winner or Defeated Side, Land or Naval:[/b] Survivors can be abandoned retainers from a routed lord's army or wounded veterans forgotten by a victorious army rushing after loot, across both land and naval wreckages.
[*][b]Gradual Casualty Scaling:[/b] Requires at least one real NPC Lord on either side of the battle (player battles excluded). Spawn chance scales with total battle casualties, reaching [b]100% at 1,500+ casualties[/b] (configurable).
[*][b]Wounded Start & +100 Relation:[/b] Rescued survivors start heavily wounded ([b]15%-20% HP[/b]) and immediately gain [b]+100 personal relation[/b] with you whether recruited or sent to town.
[*][b]Tavern Reunion & Free Recruitment:[/b] If you cannot take them right away, they travel to the nearest town tavern and roam Calradia like normal wanderers (protected from Native's random wanderer purge). Meet them later in any tavern for a custom reunion dialogue and recruit them for [b]0 gold[/b].
[*][b]World FIFO Cap & Custom Name Pools:[/b] Unrecruited survivors in the world are capped at [b]5[/b] by default (oldest is retired when exceeded) to prevent save bloat. Uses native culture-matched Wanderer templates for varied faces, hair, beards, and gear, paired with gender-aware custom culture name & epithet pools in [i]config.json[/i].
[*][b]Optional MCM v5 + JSON Fallback:[/b] Full instant hot-reload MCM support (including [b]Allow Exceeding Companion Limit[/b]), with automatic fallback to [i]Modules\NL4D\config.json[/i].
[*][b]Languages:[/b] Full [b]English (EN)[/b] and [b]Turkish (TR)[/b] localization.
[/list]
